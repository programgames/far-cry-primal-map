# Using the map

The interface is in English and French. It follows the language of your browser; the **English** /
**Français** button at the bottom of the side panel changes it. The French labels are given in *italics*.

## Progress

- The top of the side panel shows the **game progress** (the same % as in the game menu) and your play
  time.
- On the map, everything you have done is **greyed out with a green tick**.
- The map updates by itself a few seconds after the game saves (autosaves happen after missions,
  captures, fast travel…). Click **⟳** to re-read the save at once.

What counts as *done* depends on the element:

| Element | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| Collectibles (Daysha hands, Wenja bracelets, Izila masks, spirit totems, cave paintings, hunter caches) | picked up |
| Bonfires, outposts, forts, village | taken (fast travel unlocked) |
| Lost caves, camp fires, named places | discovered |
| Side missions, village missions, Beast Master hunts | completed |

Animal areas only show where animals live: they have no done/not done state.

## Filters tab (*Filtres*)

- Tick or untick a category to show or hide it. Animal areas (*Zones de chasse*) are hidden at first.
- **Hide done elements** (*Masquer les éléments faits*): show only what is left.
- **Darken unexplored areas** (*Assombrir les zones inexplorées*): the parts of Oros you have never
  visited become dark (fog of war from your save).

## Places tab (*Lieux*)

Search by name or category. Elements still to do come first. Click one to go to it on the map.

## Missions tab (*Missions*)

All story and side missions, grouped by story line (Sayla, Tensay, Wogah…), in game order. Click **⌖**
to show the mission giver on the map.

## Skills tab (*Compétences*)

The skill trees (one per character) with the skills you have learned, then the crafting upgrades you
have made.
