# Privacy

- The app **reads** your Far Cry Primal save files. It never writes to them.
- Everything runs on your PC. The map server only accepts connections from your own computer
  (`127.0.0.1`).
- The only network request: at start, the app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. No personal
  data is sent. Without internet, the map works the same.
- The app stores one setting, the save folder you chose (if any), in `%APPDATA%\OrosMap\config.json`.
- Your browser remembers your display choices (filters, open tab) for this page.
