# Installation

You need Windows 10 or 11 and Far Cry Primal (any PC version: Ubisoft Connect, Steam or Epic).
Nothing else to install.

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/programgames/far-cry-primal-map/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, click **`OrosMap-x.y.z-windows.zip`** to download it (about 45 MB).
3. Open your *Downloads* folder, right-click the zip file and choose **Extract All…**, then **Extract**.
   Keep all the files together: `OrosMap.exe` needs the `app` and `runtime` folders next to it.
4. In the extracted folder, double-click **`OrosMap.exe`**.
5. A black window opens, then the map opens in your web browser.

**Keep the black window open** while you use the map. Close it to stop the map.

Tip: right-click `OrosMap.exe` → *Show more options* → *Send to* → *Desktop (create shortcut)* to start
it from your desktop.

## "Windows protected your PC"

Windows shows this warning for programs that are not signed with a paid certificate. Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only need to do this once.

## Updating

When a new version exists, an orange banner appears at the top of the map. Click it, download the new
zip, and extract it in place of the old folder (your settings are kept: they are stored in
`%APPDATA%\OrosMap`).

## Uninstalling

Delete the folder. Optionally delete `%APPDATA%\OrosMap` (it only holds the chosen save folder).
